YouTube Streaming Guide

Fan streaming tips for supporting the Heartbound pilot trailer on YouTube.

These are fan-community streaming recommendations and are not official YouTube guidelines.

▶

How to Stream

  • ♥Search “Heartbound สุดทางหัวใจ” manually on YouTube.
  • ✓Watch the video on the official GMMTV YouTube channel.
  • ✓Watch the full video.
  • ✓Use 720p+ quality when possible.
  • ✓Keep YouTube volume above 50%. Earphones/headphones are fine.
  • ♥Like the video and leave a genuine comment.
  • ✓Watch 2–3 other PerthSanta videos before replaying the Heartbound pilot.
✕

Avoid These

  • ✕Don’t mute the video.
  • ✕Don’t speed up the video.
  • ✕Don’t rely on Autoplay when actively streaming.
  • ✕Avoid using Incognito Mode for streaming.
  • ✕Don’t stream the same video in multiple tabs at the same time.
  • ✕Don’t repeatedly refresh the page or immediately replay the video.
  • ✕Don’t spam identical comments.
☰

Busy? Use a Playlist

Busy studying, working, sleeping, or doing something else? Use one of our PerthSanta streaming playlists so you can keep supporting while you can’t actively choose videos yourself.

Our playlists mix the Heartbound pilot with other PerthSanta content for easier passive streaming.

Keep Your Engagement Genuine

  • Write comments related to Heartbound, PerthSanta, the acting, story, visuals, chemistry, or things you genuinely enjoyed.
  • Avoid posting the exact same comment repeatedly.
  • Share the official pilot link with other fans and friends.
  • Genuine engagement is always better than repetitive spam.
