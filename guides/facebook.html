Facebook Streaming Guide

How to support official PerthSanta content on Facebook.

These are fan-community streaming recommendations and are not official platform rules unless explicitly stated otherwise.

✓

How to Support

  • ✓Search for the relevant official page or use the official link provided for the current target content.
  • ✓Unmute the video.
  • ✓Watch for at least 3 seconds; longer natural viewing is encouraged.
  • ♥Like or react to the post.
  • ♥Leave a positive, relevant comment.
  • ✓Share the official post.
  • ✓Share it to your Facebook Story.

Sharing tip: if you want the post to reach more people, make sure the audience of your shared post allows others to see it.

SEE CURRENT CAMPAIGN
♥

Engagement Tips

  • ✓React or comment genuinely rather than out of routine.
  • ✓Share official posts directly rather than reuploads.
  • ✓Follow official pages to catch new posts early.
  • ✓Encourage friends and family who enjoy PerthSanta to check the post out too.
✕

Avoid

  • ✕Don’t spam the same comment repeatedly.
  • ✕Don’t repeatedly share the same post in a disruptive way.
  • ✕Don’t use bots or automated engagement.
  • ✕Don’t leave unrelated comments.

Support Genuinely

  • A real reaction, comment, or share from an actual fan always helps more than repeated mechanical activity.
