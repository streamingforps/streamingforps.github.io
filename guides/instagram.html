Instagram Engagement Guide

A guide to engaging with PerthSanta content on Instagram.

These are fan-community streaming recommendations and are not official platform rules unless explicitly stated otherwise.

✓

How to Support

  • ✓Search for the official PerthSanta / artist account relevant to the current target content, or use the official link provided for it.
  • ✓Unmute the video when watching.
  • ✓Watch for at least 3 seconds.
  • ♥Like the post.
  • ♥Leave a meaningful and positive comment.
  • ✓Repost the video when the feature is available.
  • ✓Save / bookmark the post.
  • ✓Share the post to your Instagram Story.
  • ✓Share the post to other social media platforms.
SEE CURRENT CAMPAIGN
♥

Engagement Tips

  • ✓Write comments related to the actual content rather than generic spam.
  • ✓Share official posts directly.
  • ✓Save posts you genuinely want to revisit.
  • ✓Use Stories to help other fans discover the official content.
✕

Avoid

  • ✕Don’t spam identical comments.
  • ✕Don’t paste the same message repeatedly.
  • ✕Don’t flood unrelated official posts with campaign comments.
  • ✕Don’t use automated engagement tools.

Engage For Real

  • Genuine likes, comments, and shares from real fans help far more than repetitive or automated activity.
