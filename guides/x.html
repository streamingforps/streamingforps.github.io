X Engagement Guide

How to support PerthSanta campaigns through genuine engagement on X.

These are fan-community streaming recommendations and are not official platform rules unless explicitly stated otherwise. X has explicit anti-spam and authenticity policies, so this guide is written with those in mind.

✓

How to Support

  • ✓Follow official PerthSanta accounts relevant to the current project.
  • ♥Like official campaign posts.
  • ✓Repost official posts.
  • ✓Quote Post when you genuinely have something to add.
  • ♥Reply with relevant, natural comments.
  • ✓Share official links.
  • ✓Search the current campaign keyword or hashtag to find relevant posts.
  • ✓Join current fandom conversations naturally.

Hashtags

  • ✓Use the current hashtag or keyword when relevant.
  • ✓Keep hashtags relevant to the actual post.
  • ✕Avoid stuffing posts with many unrelated hashtags.
  • ✓A small number of relevant hashtags is better than excessive hashtag use.

X currently recommends no more than 2 hashtags per post as a general best practice.

SEE CURRENT CAMPAIGN
♥

Genuine Engagement

  • ✓Write varied posts instead of copy-pasting the exact same sentence repeatedly.
  • ✓Replies should relate to the post you are replying to.
  • ✓Add your own reaction when Quote Posting.
  • ✓Engage with official and fan posts naturally.
  • ✓Space out repetitive campaign activity rather than posting identical content in bulk.
✕

Avoid

  • ✕Don’t repeatedly post identical or near-identical posts.
  • ✕Don’t spam identical replies.
  • ✕Don’t use unrelated trending hashtags just to gain visibility.
  • ✕Don’t use multiple accounts to coordinate identical engagement.
  • ✕Don’t use bots or automated engagement tools to artificially inflate activity.
  • ✕Don’t post large amounts of unsolicited mentions or replies.

These points are especially important because they align with X’s current authenticity and spam policies.

Keep it human.

  • Relevant, varied and genuine posts are better for the fandom — and safer for your account — than repetitive spam.
