Spotify Streaming Guide

Fan streaming tips for PerthSanta music and related releases.

These are fan-community streaming recommendations and are not official platform rules unless explicitly stated otherwise.

✓

General Tips

  • ✓Search for the target song manually sometimes.
  • ✓Use playlists that include the target songs.
  • ✓Keep streaming playlists reasonably varied rather than containing only one repeated track.
  • ✓Mix targeted songs with filler songs / other artists.
  • ✓Interact with Spotify naturally while listening.
  • ♥Like / save PerthSanta songs you genuinely enjoy.
  • ✓Share songs and playlists on social media.
♫

Active Streaming Tips

  • ✕Avoid looping one track continuously.
  • ✕Avoid repeating the exact same playlist over and over.
  • ✓Switch between different playlists occasionally.
  • ✓Include other songs between repeated target songs.
  • ✕Avoid muting the device while actively streaming.
  • ✕Avoid modified Spotify apps or other unofficial methods.

These are fan-community recommendations, not officially documented Spotify counting rules — we don’t make claims about exact account limits, Premium-stream filtering, volume thresholds, or playlist-hour limits.
