Fan streaming tips for supporting PerthSanta content on YouTube.

▶

How to Support

Whether you're supporting a trailer, episode clip, MV, OST, song, interview, or any other official PerthSanta upload, the same core habits help.

  • ✓Search for the target video manually on YouTube.
  • ✓Watch the official upload from the relevant official channel, at 720p+ quality.
  • ✓Watch the full video with volume above 50%.
  • ♥Like it and leave a genuine comment related to the content you're watching.

Busy? Use a Streaming Playlist

Studying, working, sleeping, or otherwise can't actively choose videos yourself? Use a PerthSanta streaming playlist instead, so you can keep supporting passively.
