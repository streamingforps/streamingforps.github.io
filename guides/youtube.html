Public Views — In Detail

Helping increase the visible YouTube view count

Starting August 24, 2026, a public view is counted as soon as the video starts playing from the first frame — across regular videos, Shorts, livestreams, and other YouTube video formats. In practice, this means:

  • The video beginning to play can count as a public view.
  • There's no longer a minimum watch-time requirement simply for the public view counter.
  • Public views may therefore rise faster than they did before.
  • This visible number mainly reflects exposure and playback starts — not deep engagement.

Just seeing a thumbnail without interacting with it is still only an impression, not a view. But if a preview or autoplay actually begins playing the video, it can count as a public view from that first frame.

⏱

Engaged Views — In Detail

Helping show real viewer interest and support

Engaged views reflect viewers who continue watching beyond the initial first frame, or who actively click or tap to watch. Even though a public view can now count immediately, if you genuinely want to support PerthSanta content, keep watching — engagement is still what matters most for the algorithm, for creators, and for real support.

  • Watch naturally, without skipping right away.
  • Continue watching beyond the opening seconds instead of stopping right after playback starts.
  • Ideally, watch a meaningful portion — or the full video if you're enjoying it.
  • Interact naturally with the content as you watch.
  • Keep likes, comments, and shares genuine and relevant.
  • Longer watch time and retention still matter for how a video performs.
  • Recommendation and analytics systems (like CTR, average view duration, and retention) still rely heavily on engaged viewing behavior.
  • Creator earnings and YouTube Partner Program metrics are still based on qualified, engaged viewing — not the new public view number alone.

What Not To Do

  • Don’t encourage artificial or automated traffic — YouTube still validates traffic and can remove invalid or artificial views even after they've been counted.
  • Don’t use bots, auto-refresh tools, scripts, or any form of artificial traffic.
  • Repetitive, unnatural viewing behavior can still be filtered out.
  • Genuine viewing and real engagement remain the safest way to support PerthSanta.

Streaming tips are community recommendations used by fans and should not be interpreted as official YouTube view-counting rules.
