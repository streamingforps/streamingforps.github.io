TikTok Streaming Guide

How to support PerthSanta content and campaigns on TikTok.

These are fan-community streaming recommendations and are not official platform rules unless explicitly stated otherwise.

✓

How to Support

  • ✓Find the official upload or account for the content you're supporting.
  • ✓Watch the target video for at least 3–5 seconds.
  • ♥Like the video.
  • ♥Leave a positive and relevant comment.
  • ✓Save the video.
  • ✓Share the video to other social media platforms.
  • ✓Share the video to your TikTok Story when relevant.
SEE CURRENT CAMPAIGN
♥

Engagement Tips

  • ✓Watch naturally rather than immediately skipping.
  • ✓Leave comments related to the actual video, PerthSanta, acting, chemistry, story, visuals, or other genuine reactions.
  • ✓Save content you genuinely want to revisit.
  • ✓Share official uploads rather than reuploads when supporting a campaign.
✕

Avoid

  • ✕Don’t spam identical comments.
  • ✕Don’t repeatedly post unrelated comments just to increase activity.
  • ✕Don’t use multiple accounts in a coordinated way to artificially inflate engagement.
  • ✕Don’t use bots or automated engagement.

Watch Naturally

  • This is a fan streaming guide, not an official explanation of TikTok’s algorithm.
  • Genuine, natural engagement always matters more than repetitive activity.
